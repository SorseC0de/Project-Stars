# Project Stars Kit Descriptors

> The three kit descriptors used to convey each sign at a glance — Complex, Mover, and a third word for simple that is not yet chosen.


Assigned 2026-08-15. Three descriptors shown in **marketing materials** and in
**each sign's introductory splash**, to convey what a kit is at a glance. Feeds
into a planned redesign of the selection screen, which currently does a poor job
of conveying kits.

## The tags

| tag | signs |
|---|---|
| **Complex** | Libra, Gemini, Leo, Capricorn, Pisces, **Aquarius** (after its rework) |
| **Mover** | Pisces, Virgo, Cancer, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Aries, Aquarius |
| **Steady** | Aries, Taurus, Virgo |
| **Chance** | Leo, Sagittarius, Capricorn |
| **Tenacious** | Taurus, Cancer, Scorpio, Libra, Gemini |

Pairs settled so far:

| sign | reads as |
|---|---|
| Aries | **Steady Mover** — the Zodiaction is what makes him a mover |
| Sagittarius | **Chance Mover** |
| Leo | **Chance + Complex** |
| Virgo | **Steady Mover** |
| Pisces | **Complex Mover** — the reworks plus two different plane playstyles |
| Taurus | **Steady + Tenacious** |
| Cancer | **Tenacious Mover** |
| Scorpio | **Tenacious Mover** |
| Libra | **Complex + Tenacious** |
| Aquarius | **Complex Mover** (after its rework) |
| Capricorn | **Complex + Chance** |
| Gemini | **Complex + Tenacious** — a lost half does not end the run, so the
sign effectively carries four lives, though a separated Gemini drops to a vanilla
**Steady** piece until reunited |

**All twelve assigned, and every one landed on exactly two tags** — twenty-four
slots filled with no sign left short and none wanting a third.

The whole vocabulary came to **five words**: Complex, Steady, Chance, Tenacious,
Mover. No further nouns were needed — *Trader* for Capricorn was proposed and
dropped, since what the shop offers is a roll, which **Chance** already says.

Cancer and Scorpio are **"baby movers"** — movers, but the gentle end of it. With
Aries and Taurus that makes the practical on-ramp four signs rather than two.

## The rule for classifying

**Complexity is what the player has to *manage*, not what happens to them.**

Sagittarius has two luck passives and was initially misfiled as Complex on a
count of systems. That is wrong: luck requires no decisions, it lands on you, and
it is *fun* for newcomers rather than demanding. Count decisions, not mechanics.

Capricorn is Complex because Pentacles stop being pickups and become currency
with a purse, a shop and a spend decision — a whole economy the player runs.

## The third word — **Steady**

Chosen 2026-08-15, and it means **predictability**, not gentleness: *you know
what you are going to get every session*. Aries is the model — no luck rolls, no
economy, no accumulating state, the same sign this session as last.

That makes it a real axis rather than a polite word for *simple*. Sagittarius is
the opposite pole — all luck, high swing — and is filed **Mover**, not Steady,
even though nothing in it is hard to manage.

Rejected along the way:

- **Balanced** — briefly chosen, then dropped. Libra **is** the balance (the
  scales, the flanking trenches, the whole sign), and in game-dev usage
  "balanced" means correctly tuned, implying the others are not.
- **Direct** — reads as *stern*, telling the player off.
- **Clear** — collides with "clear a hole" in Scorpio's vault.
- **Natural**, **Focused**, **Clean**, **Pure**, **Classic** — considered.
- **Simple**, **Easy**, **Basic** — patronising. **Essential** — implies the
  others are optional.

Rejected: *Simple*, *Easy*, *Basic* (patronising), and *Essential* (implies the
others are optional).

## Settled: up to two tags, read as a phrase

**A sign carries up to two**, and between them they describe the character —
*"Aries is a **Steady Mover**", because of his Zodiaction.*

They compose because the set has a **grammar**:

- **Adjectives — how it plays:** Complex, Steady, Chance, Tenacious.
- **Nouns — what it does:** Mover, and more to come.

So the earlier mismatch between "Complex" and "Mover" is not a fault to fix, it
*is* the structure, and the pair reads as English: *Steady Mover*, *Chance
Mover*.

**Adjective-plus-noun is the common shape, not a rule.** Leo is **Chance +
Complex** — two adjectives, no noun. Two tags from one pool, whichever two say
the most.

### What is still missing

**More nouns.** Capricorn is neither a Mover nor a Maintainer — he is a
**Trader**. Leo is a **Summoner**. Gemini may want one of its own.

**A third adjective was needed and is now in: Chance.** Sagittarius fit neither
Steady nor Complex — all luck means unpredictable, but nothing about him is
demanding to manage. *Chance* covers it, and is better than *Lucky* or *Swingy*
because it does not promise the variance is in your favour.

### The endurance group wanted an adjective, not a noun

Taurus, Cancer, Scorpio and Libra obviously belonged together, and every **noun**
proposed for them strained — *Maintainer*, *Keeper*, *Warden*, *Guardian* all
describe protecting **something else**, so Scorpio reviving *himself* kept
falling out of the category.

**Tenacious** is the answer because it describes the sign persisting rather than
the thing being protected: Scorpio comes back, Taurus endures, Cancer shelters,
Libra mends. No stretch on any of the four.

**Survivor** was never a candidate: survival is the *win condition*, so it
distinguishes nothing.
