# Zodiaction Charge Balance

> Signs with no manual Zodiaction charge are balanced that way on purpose, not unfinished.


In Project Stars, a sign having **no** manual charge source — meter filling only
from Pentacles — is a deliberate balance lever, not missing work. It is the quiet
nerf applied to board-control and passive-heavy signs, whose strength is spread
across every turn instead of concentrated in a super. As of 2026-08-10 that is
Aquarius, Capricorn, Gemini, Libra, Taurus and Virgo.

The inverse is equally deliberate: Pisces charges from two passives and so nearly
always has its Zodiaction up, but Surging Stream does very little. The trade is
the point — Pisces' game is living rent-free on Astra, not popping supers.

**Why:** the meter is only one of several places a sign's power can live, and
levelling charge rates across the twelve would flatten exactly the differences
the roster exists to create.

**How to apply:** never add a charge rule to a sign just because the others have
one, and do not file its absence as a TODO. Ask what that sign's strength already
is first. See [[project-stars-architecture]] and the note on
`Zodiaction.meterGain`.
