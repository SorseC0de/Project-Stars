# Forced Risk Should Not Be Punished

> A recurring tester complaint and the design rule it produced — the board must not force a risk and then charge for taking it.


Raised 2026-08-16, from tester feedback.

**The complaint:** a Pentacle spawns on a crumbling tile, the player has to take
it, and then falls through — punished for a risk the board required of them.

**The rule it produced:** where the game forces the risk, it should carry some
of the cost. Not remove it; carry some of it.

Fixes made so far in this spirit:

- **Astral Tears** (2026-08-16) heals **the tile you are standing on** as well
  as one at random. The tile you had to reach is the tile the coin repairs.
- **Astral Breeze and Astral Brook** already served as saves from a bad square.

**Also why Tears pays twice:** the game is inspired by **Knight Move**, where a
one-tile repair was the *only* item — on a smaller grid. This board is seven
squares, so the same repair mends a proportionally smaller share of it. Paying
out twice keeps the coin worth what it was worth there.

**How to apply:** when a mechanic punishes the player for something the board
made unavoidable, treat that as a bug in the mechanic rather than as difficulty.
Look for the version that keeps the tension and drops the unfairness. See
[[project-stars-goal]] — survival is the win condition, so a death the player
could not have avoided costs the run its whole point.
